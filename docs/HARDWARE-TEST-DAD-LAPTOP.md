# Live USB test — Dad's ASUS laptop

**Nothing is installed.** This runs Bashan OS from the USB stick only.
The laptop's own system and files are not touched.

If anything goes wrong: hold the power button for ten seconds,
pull out the USB stick, and switch on again. The laptop comes back
exactly as it was.

---

## Getting it to start from the USB

Put the USB stick in **before** switching on.

Switch on, then immediately tap one key, once a second:

- **Esc** — most likely on ASUS
- **F8** — second most likely
- **F2** — opens the settings screen instead, if the others do nothing

**These keys are a best guess and must be confirmed on the machine.**
If a menu appears, choose the line with **USB** or **SanDisk** in it.

If Windows starts instead, the key was missed. Shut down and try again.

# The checklist

Work down the list. Write **yes** or **no** next to each one.

## 1. It starts

Does the Bashan OS screen appear, and then the desktop?

## 2. Display

Is the picture the full size of the screen, not stretched or letterboxed?
Is the text sharp?

## 3. Wi-Fi

Click the network icon, bottom right.
Does it list your home Wi-Fi? Can it connect?

## 4. Brave

Open Brave from the bar at the bottom. Load any website.

## 5. The welcome app

Does **Welcome to Bashan OS** open by itself?
Click **Next** through every page. Does it reach the end?

## 6. Sound

Play a video. Is there sound from the speakers?
Do the volume keys work?

## 7. Bluetooth

Open Bluetooth settings. Does it find a phone or headphones?

## 8. Touchpad

Move the pointer. Tap to click. Two fingers to scroll.

## 9. Keyboard

Type a sentence. Check the letters are correct.

## 10. Keyboard backlight

Press the backlight key. Do the keys light up?

## 11. Screen brightness

Press the brightness keys. Does the screen get brighter and dimmer?

## 12. Camera

Open the camera app. Do you see yourself?

## 13. USB ports

Plug something into **each** USB port in turn. Does it appear?

## 14. Battery

Unplug the charger. Does the battery icon show a percentage?
Plug it back in. Does it say charging?

## 15. Suspend

Close the lid. Wait ten seconds. Open it.
Does it wake up and come back to the desktop?

---

## When finished

Shut down, remove the USB stick, switch on again.
The laptop should start its own system as normal.

---

## Known before you start

These are already known and are **not** faults to report:

- The login screen is plain grey and not branded.
  You will not see it in this test — the live session logs in by itself.
- Installing is not ready yet. This test is to check the hardware only.
