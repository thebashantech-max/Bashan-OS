# Cookies — Bashan OS 1.0

**DRAFT — not legal advice.** Prepared for review by a qualified lawyer.

## The operating system uses no cookies

Cookies are a web-browser mechanism. **Bashan OS itself is not a website and
sets no cookies.** Installing it, running it, and using its own programs
involves no cookies at all, and there is nothing in the system to consent to
or opt out of.

Nothing in Bashan OS reads, writes, or transmits a cookie on its own behalf.
The welcome application stores two small files in your own home folder
(whether to show itself again, and which theme you chose). Those are
ordinary files on your own computer, they are never sent anywhere, and they
are not cookies.

## Your browser is a different matter

Bashan OS includes the Brave web browser, and you may install others. **When
you visit a website, that website may set cookies in your browser**, exactly
as it would on any other operating system.

Those cookies are between you and the website. They are governed by that
site's own cookie and privacy policies. Brave's tracking protection
("Shields") blocks many third-party tracking cookies by default, but it is
not a complete defence and we make no promise about what any site does.

## The download website

**If and when Bashan OS is distributed from a website, that website may use
cookies** — for example to measure traffic, remember a language choice, or
operate a content delivery network.

That website will need its own cookie notice, and depending on where its
visitors are, a consent banner.

**A lawyer must decide**, once the download site exists and its hosting and
analytics are chosen:

1. Whether any cookie it sets is "strictly necessary" (no consent needed) or
   requires prior consent under the EU ePrivacy Directive and GDPR, the UK
   PECR, or comparable law elsewhere.
2. Whether analytics are used at all, and if so whether a consent banner is
   required before they load.
3. What the site's cookie notice must say, and where it must appear.

**That is a question about the website, not about the operating system.**
Keeping the two separate matters: a cookie notice that implies the OS tracks
you would be inaccurate and would undermine the privacy statement.
